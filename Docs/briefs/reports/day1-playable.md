# Report: day1-playable (executor Opus, 12.11.2026)

**What a player can do now (mouse only, from the first screen).** Open a pack and pick a bait. Click the bait counter on a rod card to change the bait (it cycles through held baits, then a bare hook). Drag the yellow depth handle up or down. Click START DAY. Click a rod card to pull. Every landed fish flies on its own arc into the bucket, and the bucket rocks. Watch the shipping (`+$N` above the bucket, the money counts up). Click the sea or press Space to skip it. Then PAY, use the plain shop, and click NEXT DAY. When the fee is too high: run over, then NEW RUN. The mouse wheel moves the camera along the depth.
