## 3. Technical decisions (each is revocable; say so in the report if you change one)

Start gift

1. `NewRun` opens the run in the preparation of day 1. There is no shop before day 1. The shop opens first after day 1. Remove `firstShop` or leave it always false; list what you did.
2. Start coins: 0.
3. Order at the start: the starting bait pack (as now), then the gift. `StartDay` is refused while the gift is not taken.
4. The gift offers exactly three cards, always the same: `SecondRod` (+1 rod), `Sharpen` (+2 levels on rod 1), `DeeperLine` (+1 zone). The player takes one, free. After that, shop prices follow the state exactly as if the gift had been bought (the next rod costs 8, and so on).
5. The core gives the view what it needs: a state flag "gift pending", a query for the three offers (kind, English name, English effect line), a command to take one, events for "offered" and "taken". English texts for the cards: "Second Rod - Fish with two rods from day 1.", "Sharp Rod - Rod 1 starts at level 3.", "Deeper Line - The next depth zone is open from day 1." Adjust a line if it states the effect wrongly.

Day end at the target

6. During the day, at the moment the day points first reach the norm (the same points that `SettleDay` compares with the norm), the core stores the seconds left and calls `EndDay()` at once. Everything after that works as now: all rods pull, every hooked fish lands and counts, shipping, summary. If the norm is never reached, the day runs to 0 as now.
7. The time bonus replaces the surplus bonus: `bonus = min(4, floor(5 x secondsLeftAtTarget / dayLength))` coins, where `dayLength` is the real length of that day. Put the three numbers (5 steps, 1 coin per step, cap 4) into `PayoutData` and into `economy-v2.data.json`. A day that reached the norm only during the final pull has bonus 0.
8. The most a day can pay stays bounded: base 4 + time 4 + interest 3, plus charm rows. Do not add any unbounded coin source.
9. `DaySettled` carries the time bonus and the seconds saved (and the day length), so the view can write "23 s saved". Rename `payoutOver` or keep it; list every public name you changed. Add a query the view can call during the day: the bonus the player would get if the norm were reached now.
10. Tide Pool keeps its shape on the new bonus: "After each day, +1 more coin for each full 20% of the day you saved, up to +4." Update `ruleEn` and `ruleRu` in `Docs/design/modifiers-v2.data.json` (Russian: «После каждого дня: ещё +1 монета за каждые полные 20% сэкономленного дня, не больше +4.»), regenerate the rows, fix the tests.
11. Gold Sunfish: no guard. A day may end before it arrives. Measure how often (block 4).

Balance

12. Change no norm, no price, no charm value. The only number changes are decisions 2, 7 and 10.

Bots

13. Each build bot takes the gift that fits its ground targets (two or more rods wanted: `SecondRod`; `lone_rod`: `Sharpen`; a build that wants a deeper line: `DeeperLine`; otherwise `SecondRod`). The person-like bot is measured three times, once per gift.
